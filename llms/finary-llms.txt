# Finary

> Finary is a French fintech platform for personal wealth tracking and management. It aggregates a user's entire net worth — stocks, funds, ETFs, cryptocurrency, real estate, savings, life insurance, startups and alternative assets — into one dashboard by connecting to 20,000+ banks and brokers. It adds fee detection, allocation and diversification analysis, dividend tracking, budgeting, in-app crypto trading/staking and life-insurance products. 1M+ users; regulated in France by the AMF and ACPR. Finary publishes NO official public API or SDK; app frontends use a private, undocumented API that the community has reverse-engineered.

## Product
- [Finary website](https://finary.com): Follow, optimize and invest your wealth in one place.
- [Pricing](https://finary.com/en/pricing): Finary Lite (free), Finary Plus (EUR 149.99/yr), Finary Pro.
- [Security](https://finary.com/en/security): TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest, read-only bank access via Plaid/Powens/Flanks, third-party pen tests, private bug bounty.
- [Sign up](https://app.finary.com/v2/signup) / [Log in](https://app.finary.com/v2/login)
